## 🎨 Working With Visual Effects and Chroma Key

To apply an effect, drag it from the effects library onto the clip on your timeline. A short preview plays automatically. Use the settings panel to adjust intensity, speed, and direction. If you change your mind, remove the effect by selecting it and pressing delete, or undo the last action with the standard keyboard shortcut.

For chroma key work, place your green-screen clip on the timeline, open the chroma key tool, and use the color picker to select the background color you want removed. Fine-tune the tolerance slider until the background disappears cleanly without eating into your subject. Then place your replacement video or image on a track below the clip so it shows through the removed area.

## 🎙️ Screen Recording and Voiceover Workflow

Screen recording and voiceover tools are designed to work together. Start a screen recording, choose your capture area, and enable your microphone. When the recording finishes, the file is added to your media library automatically. Drag it to the timeline and use the voiceover tool to add narration to specific moments. This two-step workflow keeps your original recording untouched while you build a polished final product.

For the clearest results, record in a quiet room, close unnecessary browser tabs, and test your microphone level before capturing a long session.
